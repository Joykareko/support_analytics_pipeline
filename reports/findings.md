# Headline findings (synthetic data, Oct 2025 to Sep 2026)

1. Payment provider outage: contacts hit 31.1 per 1,000 orders against a normal 17.2. First response SLA fell from 91% to 79%, CSAT from 75% to 57%, and the backlog peaked at 279 open tickets against a normal 115. The daily alert flagged exactly the 3 outage days and nothing else all year.
2. Chatbot: across 20,277 conversations the bot claimed 46% resolved, but only 31% were truly resolved. For refunds the gap is 46% claimed vs 21% true; for key activation it is small (45% vs 40%). Cost per contact fell from EUR 3.39 to EUR 2.89 after launch, but contacts per 1,000 orders did not fall (16.7 before, 17.2 after), because many "resolved" customers came back.
3. Help article: after the key activation article was rewritten, readers who still contacted support fell from 22% to 9%, and key activation contacts fell from 4.32 to 2.85 per 1,000 orders.
4. Seller disputes: up from 1.16 to 1.92 per 1,000 orders (+65%). They are the most expensive contact (EUR 6.92 each vs EUR 3.08 average) with the lowest CSAT (59%).
5. Tagging: 31% of tickets from agents who joined in July are untagged, against 9% for existing agents.
6. Forecast: planned orders x recent contact rate gives a 4.7% average weekly error, against 9.2% for "same as last week". Next week needs about 1,459 agent contacts, 421 agent hours, or 12 full time agents.
